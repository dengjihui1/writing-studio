# Quality gates

## Quick gate

Use for low-stakes or short-form work.

- The result is independently deliverable from one draft; no detector report, historical version, or author sample was treated as required input.
- The output directly satisfies the requested operation, genre, language, and format.
- The main point is visible early enough for the medium.
- Every paragraph has a distinct job.
- No facts, quotations, citations, credentials, or experiences were invented.
- Meaning and evidentiary strength were preserved during editing or translation.
- The prose sounds genuinely authored: specific, logically continuous, and not uniformly generic or mechanically patterned.
- No chatbot residue, fake quotations, invented personal detail, or deliberate imperfection was introduced.
- Grammar, punctuation, terminology, and length are consistent.

## Full gate

Use for long-form, assessed, published, professional, or high-stakes writing.

### Contract

- Audience, purpose, medium, source boundary, voice, and change authority are satisfied.
- When the user supplied only one draft, the chosen edit depth came from the draft's actual risks rather than the absence of reports or prior versions.

### Argument and evidence

- The controlling idea is specific and sustained.
- Each material claim has supplied or verified support.
- Evidence is interpreted rather than merely listed.
- Counterevidence, limitations, uncertainty, and alternatives are treated proportionally.
- Conclusions do not exceed the evidence.

### Structure

- Each section and paragraph performs necessary work.
- Order follows reader need, not the order in which material was supplied.
- Transitions express real logical relationships.
- Opening and ending perform different, useful functions.
- Adjacent paragraphs are not freely interchangeable; each one accumulates, contests, narrows, or redirects what came before.
- Long paragraphs have one governing question and no visible seam from mechanical merging.
- The distribution of space reflects argumentative importance rather than equal coverage of every category.
- Where a case spine is used, it advances across the paper and is not repeated as decorative context.

### Prose and voice

- Sentences are clear without flattening nuance.
- Concrete language replaces vagueness only when support exists.
- Terminology, point of view, tense, spelling variant, formality, and tone are stable.
- Repetition is purposeful; rhythm is varied; emphasis is earned.
- When author samples exist, the output reflects their stable tendencies without imitating surface quirks mechanically.
- When no sample exists, the voice is restrained and genre-appropriate; it does not invent a persona.
- Structural variation follows meaning rather than random sentence-length manipulation.
- Paragraph length follows conceptual movement rather than a target range or deliberate irregularity.
- Paragraph openings and endings do substantive work instead of repeating a template.
- Consecutive paragraphs do not repeat the same claim-evidence-qualification-summary sequence.
- Citations perform identifiable work and are not processed through identical source-summary-limit formulas.
- Conventional devices such as passive voice, hedging, lists, dashes, and parallelism survive when functional and are revised only when habitual or empty.
- Persistent paragraphs do not retain a prompt-shaped define-list-balance-conclude ancestry.
- Nominalisations, participial add-ons, and coordinated noun phrases do not accumulate to the point that actors, actions, or sequence disappear.
- Persistent sections are not composed of uniformly self-contained answer cards; entry moves, citation staging, and paragraph endings vary because the reasoning varies.
- Where a claim legitimately develops across paragraphs, the dependency is visible and the later paragraph resolves or changes the issue carried forward.

### Human-writing audit

- The first pass diagnosed and repaired document-level template ancestry before sentence-level variation; it did not reserve deep reconstruction for a later detector-guided round.
- No generic throat-clearing, chatbot framing, process narration, or unsolicited offer remains in the deliverable.
- No inflated significance, vague authority, unsupported consensus, marketing superlative, or generic optimistic conclusion substitutes for evidence.
- No repeated contrast formula, forced triad, preview sentence, pseudo-profound fragment, or canned transition dominates the prose.
- Sentence and paragraph rhythms have meaningful variation, without sacrificing readability or genre convention.
- The revision was compared with the source for factual, causal, tonal, and certainty drift.
- Humanization was not framed as detector evasion and no detector outcome or fabricated AI percentage was promised.
- The writer's judgement is visible in selection and unequal emphasis; the paper does not read as exhaustive rubric coverage.
- The conclusion resolves the governing question without reciting a full inventory of earlier sections.

### Detector-feedback integrity

Apply when a detector report, highlight map, or before/after percentage informed the revision.

- Report provider, score, segment count, qualifying-text count, and version differences are recorded when available.
- Highlighted spans are classified as protected, conditional, editable, or mixed before changes are planned.
- Exact quotations, participant testimony, numbers, and fixed source language were not altered to influence a detector.
- The report-wide percentage is not confused with an editable-author-prose measure, and any protected-text floor is disclosed when material.
- Exited, persistent, entered, and unmeasured passages are distinguished; an overall score is not treated as a complete diagnosis.
- Substantial unhighlighted passages from the same version are used as negative-space controls when available.
- Layout, surface, structural, and epistemic changes are separated before drawing conclusions.
- Claimed lessons rely on recurring passage evidence and use calibrated language such as “suggests” or “correlates with”.
- The revision rebuilt persistent structural patterns instead of only swapping vocabulary or merging paragraphs.
- A cluster that survived a previous substantive rewrite received a discourse reset rather than another sentence-level paraphrase.
- A comparable flat or worse result triggered a change in intervention class; the failed class was not repeated with more intensity.
- Persistent clusters were drafted from a ledger and materially new section-function sequence, then checked against the source for fidelity.
- When a prior pass produced only modest improvement, exited editable passages were compared with persistent editable passages, and any lesson was based on repeated functional differences rather than copied surface features.
- The remaining highlighted share of protected quotations and conventional language was separated from editable author prose before further intervention was chosen.
- Reported and editable word counts were treated as controls. No lesson equates lower AI-writing signal with shorter prose, and any substantial content reduction has an independent editorial justification.
- If an AI-writing report and a similarity report were both supplied, their percentages, submission metadata, match categories, and purposes remained separate.
- Similarity was reviewed for citation, quotation, source dependence, and integrity issues; zero similarity was not treated as a target or badge of quality.
- A visual highlight spanning quotation and analysis was split by ownership before conclusions were drawn, and representative mappings were checked against page images.
- If the user requested learning only or ended further document revision, the source artifact remained untouched.
- No deliberate error, fake anecdote, random burstiness, invisible character, translation loop, or detector-bypass tactic was introduced.
- Success is judged by argument, evidence, voice, and fidelity; a detector percentage is supplementary and never guaranteed.

### Integrity

- User-provided facts, sourced facts, inference, and suggestion remain distinguishable.
- Quotes and citations are exact and traceable when used.
- Personal authorship and experiences have not been fabricated.
- Sensitive or high-stakes claims are appropriately qualified.

### Delivery

- The finished text appears before process commentary.
- The first delivered revision is complete and usable; it is not an intentionally shallow intermediate draft waiting for detector feedback.
- Alternatives are limited to decisions where choice adds value.
- Remaining assumptions or evidence gaps are visible and concise.

## Claim-evidence audit format

Use when risk warrants it:

| Claim | Support | Status | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Concise claim | Source, supplied evidence, or none | supported / partial / unsupported | keep / qualify / verify / remove |
