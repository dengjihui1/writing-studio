# Writing Studio

Produce clear, credible, genuinely authored writing while preserving facts, evidence boundaries, intent, and voice.

One draft is enough. A detector report, previous version, or author sample may improve diagnosis, but none is required for a strong first result. Humanization is editorial quality control, never detector evasion.

## 0. Choose one execution mode

Read [references/intake-and-routing.md](references/intake-and-routing.md) and route before choosing edit depth.

| Mode | Use when | Core behaviour | Default output |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mode A — Direct writing / single-draft** | The user supplies ordinary writing material, one current draft, no reports, or only one report without a comparable before-and-after pair | Draft or revise directly from the available material. Diagnose the prose itself and produce the strongest justified first result; never wait for historical versions or detector feedback. | Finished text or artifact, plus a brief change note when useful |
| **Mode B — Comparative report learning** | The user supplies comparable before and after drafts with their first and second AI-writing reports, or explicitly asks to learn from a measured revision pair | Map what exited, persisted, entered, or became unmeasured; distinguish correlated editorial improvements from layout or measurement effects; transfer only supported lessons into the next revision. | Comparison diagnosis, finished next revision when requested, and a concise fidelity note |

Mode B is an enhancement, not a prerequisite for Mode A. Do not ask a normal user for a previous draft or detector report. One report may guide local inspection inside Mode A, but it cannot support before-and-after attribution.

Within Mode B, choose the requested action:

- **learn-and-revise:** diagnose the measured pair, then revise the current version using supported lessons;
- **learn-only:** freeze the document and update or report the reusable editorial lessons;
- **diagnose-only:** explain the comparison without silently changing the prose.

## 1. Establish the contract

Read [references/intake-and-routing.md](references/intake-and-routing.md). Infer what is safe to infer and ask only when a missing answer would materially change the result.

Capture:

- operation, genre, audience, purpose, language, medium, length, deadline, and output format;
- evidence boundary and whether external research is allowed or required;
- voice evidence, if any;
- change authority: expression-only, light, substantive, or full rewrite;
- content budget: claims, examples, citations, qualifications, protected text, and approximate development that must remain.

The user's explicit change authority controls the intervention. Do not turn a request to improve expression into an unauthorized rewrite of the argument.

## 2. Route to the smallest sufficient workflow

Read only the references needed for the request:

- Genre conventions: the relevant section of [references/genre-playbooks.md](references/genre-playbooks.md).
- New drafting or substantial rebuilding: [references/drafting.md](references/drafting.md).
- Editing, polishing, translation, or critique: [references/editing.md](references/editing.md).
- A single formulaic draft requiring deep humanization: [references/first-pass-reconstruction.md](references/first-pass-reconstruction.md).
- Any drafted or materially revised prose: [references/human-writing.md](references/human-writing.md).
- Mode B, or any supplied AI-writing highlights, similarity reports, or comparable versions: [references/detector-feedback.md](references/detector-feedback.md).

Do not load every reference by default.

## 3. Build or recover the evidence spine

Before drafting or deep rewriting:

1. state the controlling idea or reader promise;
2. inventory claims, evidence, examples, scenes, citations, limits, and constraints;
3. classify text as protected, conditionally editable, or author prose;
4. give each section and paragraph a necessary job;
5. allocate space by argumentative importance rather than checklist symmetry;
6. draft or rebuild logic before sentence-level polish.

Never invent facts, quotations, citations, results, credentials, experiences, motives, or personal details. Mark missing support or narrow the claim.

## 4. Choose edit depth from the text and authority

- **Expression-only:** preserve argument, evidence coverage, conclusion, and approximate length; improve wording, sentence movement, citation integration, and local flow.
- **Light:** repair grammar, mechanics, awkwardness, and local clarity.
- **Substantive:** reorder or rebuild weak paragraphs and sections while preserving thesis, evidence, citation intent, and claim strength.
- **Full reconstruction:** recover the source ledger, discard inherited sentence order where needed, and rebuild a prompt-shaped or interchangeable document from its evidence.

For a heavily templated sole draft, use substantive or full reconstruction on the first pass unless the user explicitly limits authority. Do not deliberately deliver a shallow intermediate version while waiting for detector feedback.

## 5. Make the prose genuinely authored

Use [references/human-writing.md](references/human-writing.md) proportionally. Prioritize:

- concrete actors, decisions, conditions, evidence relations, and consequences;
- paragraph interiors that develop rather than list;
- section dependency instead of interchangeable mini-essays;
- citation functions that vary because the reasoning varies;
- selective emphasis and calibrated claims;
- natural target-language rhythm without artificial randomness;
- the author's stable voice when reliable samples exist.

Remove template residue, canned transitions, empty abstraction, mechanical symmetry, repeated complete endings, and chatbot commentary. Keep passive voice, hedging, technical terms, parallelism, lists, or long sentences when they perform real work.

Do not add deliberate errors, slang, fake anecdotes, invisible characters, translation loops, synonym spinning, or fabricated personality. Do not promise a detector score.

## 6. Run comparative report learning only when supported

For Mode B, follow the input contract and full comparison workflow in [references/detector-feedback.md](references/detector-feedback.md). If the pair is incomplete, state the narrower inference boundary and fall back to Mode A for any requested revision.

- Establish version comparability before attributing any movement.
- Separate protected quotations, conventional text, and editable author prose.
- Compare exited, persistent, entered, unhighlighted, and unmeasured passages.
- Inspect reasoning function, evidence order, paragraph ancestry, section dependency, and report segmentation—not isolated words or sentence lengths.
- Treat reported and body word counts as controls; never assume shorter prose is more human.
- Keep AI-writing and similarity reports separate. Review similarity for citation, quotation, source-use, and integrity issues; zero similarity is not a quality target.
- Escalate from surface editing to structural work only when the evidence and change authority justify it.
- Stop when further report-led revision would weaken evidence, protected text, required content, methodological accuracy, voice, or user intent.

If the user asks only to learn from a finished artifact, freeze the artifact and update the reusable workflow without another prose pass.

## 7. Verify fidelity and deliver

Compare the result against the source. Check names, numbers, chronology, quotations, citations, terminology, causal direction, scope, certainty, and content budget.

Default delivery:

1. finished text or requested artifact;
2. material assumptions or unresolved evidence gaps;
3. a concise change summary when it helps review.

Use the relevant artifact skill when DOCX, PDF, slides, spreadsheets, or another formatted deliverable is requested.

Before finalizing, read [references/quality-gates.md](references/quality-gates.md) and apply the proportional gate. For high-stakes work, run the full gate and a compact claim-evidence audit.

When maintaining this skill, read [references/evaluation.md](references/evaluation.md), test representative cases and changed scripts, validate the package, and promote only lessons supported by repeated evidence.
